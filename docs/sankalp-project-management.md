# Sankalp Project Management

Nyasa uses **Sankalp** as the family-facing project-management module. A Sankalp may be a funded implementation project, a research effort, an asset-management study, a business exploration, an event, or community work.

## Lifecycle

1. Concept: purpose, rules, tentative budget, tentative dates, and team are visible to the Kul.
2. Research: members collect ideas, constraints, vendors, land/business options, or feasibility notes.
3. Estimate Pending: project manager waits for a vendor, advisor, or internal estimate.
4. Estimate Received: tentative budget is replaced by the estimated/actual project budget.
5. Kosh Sangrah: members contribute and allocate funds to the Sankalp.
6. Ready for Implementation: required budget is collected.
7. Karya: implementation lead reports work, milestones, expenses, and bills.
8. Poorn: project manager closes the Sankalp with final status and completion report.

## Roles

- Project Manager: creates the Sankalp, defines rules, owns scope and milestone status.
- Progress Auditor: reviews whether progress and bills match the declared rules.
- Implementation Lead: executes or coordinates the actual work and submits field updates.
- Sadasya: views the Sankalp, contributes through Kosh, and follows reports.

## Rules

Every Sankalp should state what is covered and not covered. This prevents unclear spending later. Examples:

- Covered: material, labour, vendor advances, inspection travel, government/document fees.
- Not covered: unrelated personal travel, unapproved upgrades, undocumented cash spending.
