# Nyas Market Launch Plan

## Positioning

Nyas should not enter the market as only a family tree app. The stronger category is:

**Family OS for Indian joint families, private family trusts, ancestral assets, Kosh, Sankalp, legacy records, and family governance.**

The emotional promise:

> विरासत, परिवार, कोष, संकल्प और निर्णय - सब एक सुरक्षित डिजिटल न्यास में।

## Primary Customers

1. **Large joint families**
   Families with 50-500 members across cities who want one private place for Parichay, Kul Map, photos, events, and family communication.

2. **Ancestral-property families**
   Families managing village houses, farms, temples, dharamshalas, shops, or shared assets.

3. **Private family trusts**
   Religious, social, charitable, educational, or legacy trusts that need transparent records without a heavy ERP.

4. **Family businesses**
   Business families that need family governance, succession records, next-generation engagement, project voting, and structured decisions.

5. **NRI-linked Indian families**
   Families where overseas members want visibility, trust, transparent contribution, and digital family connection before sending money.

## First Offer

Lead with a service, not only software:

**Family Digitalization Camp**

In 7 days, Nyas helps a family create:

- Branded family homepage
- Member Parichay directory
- First Kul Map
- Photo and document archive
- First Sankalp page
- Basic Kosh setup
- Invite workflow for family members

This gives immediate value and makes annual subscription easier.

## Go-To-Market Motion

### Phase 1: Proof and Warm Network

- Use Nyasa Trust as the live demo.
- Prepare one Hindi-English product flyer.
- Prepare a 3-minute screen-recorded demo.
- Approach 10 known families through warm introductions.
- Offer 3 discounted founding family slots.

### Phase 2: Partner Channels

Approach people already advising families:

- Chartered accountants
- Family lawyers
- Wealth planners
- Family business consultants
- Trust managers
- Genealogy and history researchers
- Religious/community organizers

Partner pitch:

> Give your clients a private family portal for legacy, records, shared projects, and transparent family funds.

### Phase 3: Repeatable Sales Assets

Build:

- Demo workspace
- Pricing page
- Case study from Nyasa Trust
- WhatsApp intro message
- PDF brochure
- Onboarding checklist
- Data privacy and trust page

## Product Requirements Before Charging

Must-have:

- Mobile-first UX
- Stable login and invite flow
- Family tree merge/deactivate duplicate flow
- Downloadable Kul Map
- Clear privacy controls
- Admin onboarding dashboard
- Payment/subscription tracking
- Exportable reports for Kosh and Sankalp

Should-have:

- Guided profile completion score
- Family document vault
- Gallery albums
- Health tree
- Legacy timeline
- Demo mode for sales
